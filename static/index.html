⚡ Extension Ready to Activate

Chrome requires developer mode for extensions in testing. To activate in 10 seconds:

  1. Open a new tab and go to chrome://extensions
  2. Turn Developer mode ON in the top-right corner.
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the extension folder from your project!
💡 Or simply double-click run_chrome_with_extension.bat in your project folder to launch Chrome with Echo-Hands active automatically!
